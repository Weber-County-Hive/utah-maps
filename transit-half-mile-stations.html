MAP 010 · Wasatch Front

The Half-Mile Around Every Station

A half-mile circle around all 65 FrontRunner and TRAX stations. That is the distance the federal Build HUBS Act (S. 3636) would use to decide which homes and shops can get cheaper federal loans. Tap a station to zoom in and see whether your street is inside the circle.

Proposed, not law. S. 3636 was introduced Jan. 14, 2026 and sits in the Senate Committee on Environment and Public Works with no hearing or vote listed (checked Oct. 26, 2026). The circles show where the bill would apply if it passed as written. Bus rapid transit stops (Ogden's OGX and Utah County's UVX) and multimodal stations would also qualify; this map shows rail stations only.

What the circles mean

Each circle has a radius of half a mile (804.67 meters), drawn to scale around the station point. One circle covers about 503 acres (Hive math: π × 0.5² square miles × 640). Where stations are close together, circles overlap.

Being inside a circle does not mean a project is planned there. It means a project there could apply for the loans if the bill became law.

How this map is sourced

Station points come from OpenStreetMap, U.S. DOT and Wikipedia coordinates, the same set used on the Hive's housing case file. The bill details come from the text of S. 3636 on congress.gov. Station markers are approximate; check the station itself before relying on an exact distance.
