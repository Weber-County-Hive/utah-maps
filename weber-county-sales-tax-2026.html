Weber County's New Sales Tax

Starting Oct. 1, 2026, sales tax goes up 0.20% everywhere in Weber County. That is 20 cents on every $100 of taxable purchases. The County Commission approved it unanimously; there was no ballot question and no mailed notice. Why the rates are not all the same

Utah sales tax is built in layers. Everyone pays the state's 4.85%. Counties add local layers on top, and some cities and towns add one more of their own.

The new 0.20% is a county layer, so it is added in every part of Weber County. Riverdale and Huntsville already had an extra local layer of their own before this tax: 0.20% in Riverdale and 1.00% in Huntsville, according to the Utah State Tax Commission's rate charts. So they start higher and end higher. Everyone's increase is the same 0.20%.

Who pays, and where the money goes

Sales tax is charged where you shop, not where you live. Visitors and shoppers from other places pay it too, which the county named as an advantage.

Of the $13.1 million a year, $3.27 million goes to the cities for transportation, $3.27 million to the county for transportation or public safety, and $6.54 million to the county for any purpose through Sept. 30, 2029. After that, half goes to transportation and half to FrontRunner projects in Weber County.
