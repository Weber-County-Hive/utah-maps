Utah House districtTap to flip ↻
One of 75 areas that each elect one member of the Utah House of Representatives for a two-year term. The lines on this map were drawn after the 2020 Census and are used from 2022 to 2032.
Utah Senate districtTap to flip ↻
One of 29 areas that each elect one state senator for a four-year term. About half the Senate seats are on the ballot in any election year.
Open seatTap to flip ↻
A race with no incumbent running, because the current officeholder retired, ran for something else, or lost a primary.
IncumbentTap to flip ↻
The person who holds the office now and is running to keep it.
Campaign finance reportTap to flip ↻
The report each candidate files listing money raised and spent. State candidates file on Utah's disclosures website; each Hive case file names the reports it used.
Disclosure formTap to flip ↻
A form candidates and officeholders file listing their jobs, businesses, and other interests that could conflict with public duties. What it must list depends on the office.
PACTap to flip ↻
A political action committee: a group that collects money and gives it to candidates or spends it on elections. Many case files show how much of a campaign's money came from PACs versus individuals.
Write-in candidateTap to flip ↻
A candidate whose name is not printed on the ballot. Voters write the name in, and it counts only if the candidate filed as a write-in by the deadline.

How this map works

The races come straight from the candidates2026 docket, so a new case file shows up here automatically once it is added there.

A race that crosses county lines, such as Senate District 20, is drawn on its own district lines. County commission and council races are drawn on county lines. Congressional and statewide races are listed in the panel instead of drawn, because Utah's congressional lines were redrawn under a 2026 court order.

What "covered" means

A shaded area means the Hive has published a case file for that race. An outlined area with no shading means we haven't yet. It says nothing about the candidates in that race.

Grades in the case files use the Hive's standing rubric: a C means the record meets what Utah law requires; a B or A means it goes beyond.
